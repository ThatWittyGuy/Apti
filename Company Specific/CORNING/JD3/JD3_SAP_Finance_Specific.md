**Q1.** 🟢 What does "RTR" stand for in finance and SAP?
- A) Real-Time Reporting
- B) Record-to-Report — the end-to-end finance process from recording transactions to producing financial statements and management reports
- C) Review, Test, Release
- D) Revenue Tracking and Reporting

**Answer: B**

---

**Q2.** 🟢 What is the "Record-to-Report" (RTR) process?

- D) An IT ticketing process from issue recording to resolution

**Answer: B**
