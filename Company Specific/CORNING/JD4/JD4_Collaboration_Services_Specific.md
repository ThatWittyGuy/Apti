## SECTION A: Collaboration Services Operations & Support (35 Questions)

**Q1.** 🟢 What is a "digital workplace"?
- A) A physical office with computers on every desk
- B) A technology-enabled work environment providing employees with the tools, platforms, and connectivity to collaborate and work productively from any location
- C) A virtual reality office environment
- D) A cloud-based HR management system

**Answer: B**

**Q2.** 🟢 What is "first-level support" (L1) in IT?
- A) The most senior and specialized IT support team

- C) A support team that only handles hardware issues

---

**Q3.** 🟢 What is "second-level support" (L2) in IT?

- C) External vendor support contracted for complex issues
- D) Management-level IT staff who approve IT requests

**Q4.** 🟢 What is a "knowledge base" in IT support?
