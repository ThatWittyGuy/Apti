# Shared — Business Analysis MCQs
### Applies to: JD5 (Business Engagement), JD3 (SAP Finance), any JD mentioning stakeholders, requirements, process improvement
> 35 Questions | 🟢 Easy | 🟡 Medium | 🔴 Hard

---

**Q1.** 🟢 What is a "business analyst" (BA) and what is their primary role?
- A) A financial analyst who analyzes business profitability
- B) A professional who bridges the gap between business stakeholders and IT — eliciting, analyzing, and documenting requirements, and ensuring technology solutions address real business needs
- C) A data analyst specializing in business metrics
- D) A project manager responsible for business deliverables

**Answer: B**

---

**Q2.** 🟢 What is "requirements elicitation"?
- A) A legal process for eliciting compliance requirements
- B) The process of gathering requirements from stakeholders through interviews, workshops, observation, surveys, and document analysis — discovering what is needed before designing a solution
- C) Writing technical requirements from existing documentation
- D) Electing requirements from a prioritized backlog

**Answer: B**

---

**Q3.** 🟢 What is the difference between a "functional requirement" and a "non-functional requirement"?
- A) Functional requirements are for developers; non-functional are for testers

- C) Non-functional requirements are optional; functional are mandatory

**Q6.** 🟢 What is a "process flow" diagram?

**Q7.** 🟡 What is "gap analysis" in business analysis?

**Q14.** 🟡 What is "acceptance criteria" in agile BA?

**Q15.** 🟡 What is a "workflow automation" opportunity and how is it identified?
