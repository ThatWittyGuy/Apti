## SECTION A: Digital Innovation, AI Adoption & Business Engagement (35 Questions)

**Q1.** 🟢 What is "business engagement" in an IT context?
- A) Engaging employees in IT training programs
- B) The practice of IT professionals partnering with business stakeholders to understand their needs, translate them into technology solutions, and ensure IT initiatives deliver measurable business value
- C) Managing IT vendor engagement and contracts
- D) Engaging customers through digital marketing platforms

**Answer: B**

---

**Q2.** 🟢 What is a "use case" in the context of AI and automation?
- A) A legal case study about technology usage
- B) A specific scenario describing how a technology (AI, automation) can be applied to solve a defined business problem — including the problem, solution approach, expected benefit, and success criteria

**Q3.** 🟢 What is "process automation" in a business context?

**Q4.** 🟢 What is "digital innovation" in an enterprise?
